Galaxy Trucker Rules Summary
Game Components

Ship boards (Level testflight and II)
Component tiles including:

Structural modules
Cabins
Engines (single and double)
Cannons (single and double)
Battery components (2-3 batteries each)
Shield generators
Cargo holds (standard and special)
Life support systems for aliens

Adventure cards
Flight board
Astronaut figures
Battery tokens
Credit tokens
Goods blocks (green, blue, yellow, red)
Dice (2)
Rocket markers (2 per player)
Timer

Setup

Select game level (testflight or II) and use corresponding flight and ship boards
Place all component tiles face-down and mix them
Each player takes a ship board, 2 rocket markers, and a starting cabin
Place one rocket on the ship board, the other in the parking lot/starting space
Prepare the adventure deck according to the flight level

Ship Building
Players simultaneously build ships by following these steps:

Using one hand, take one component tile from the pile
Look at it only after bringing it to your board
Either attach it to your ship or return it face-up to the pile

Building Rules

Components must be placed adjacent to other components
Connectors must match: universal connectors can connect to any type, one-pipe connectors can only connect to one-pipe or universal, two-pipe connectors can only connect to two-pipe or universal
Smooth sides cannot connect to any connectors
All sides that touch must have legal connections
Ship must hold together at all times
Components become welded once you take another tile
All the placement conditions are evaluated in the end of the building, except
for the check if a tile is inside the board, is near another tile (not checking connectors, only nearby) and other placement basics

Special Component Rules

Engines: Must point toward the rear (toward player), nothing can be placed behind them
Cannons: Can point in any direction but most effective pointing forward, nothing can be placed in front of barrel
Double Engines/Cannons: Same placement rules as regular ones but require battery power
Shield Generators: Each covers two sides of the ship
Cargo Holds: Standard holds any goods except red, special holds can store any goods including red

Advanced Building Rules

Players can reserve up to 2 components in upper right corner of ship board
Flight forecast piles can be examined (after adding at least one component)
Timer is used to limit building time (number of flips depends on level)

Preparing for Launch

Check all ships for building violations and fix them
Place 2 humans in each cabin (without life support)
Place humans or aliens in cabins with life support systems
Fill battery components with appropriate number of tokens
Prepare the adventure deck

Flight Phase
The leader (first player) reveals adventure cards one at a time. All players resolve each card's effects before moving to the next.
Types of Adventures
Planets

Players can land to collect goods
Leader chooses first, followed by other players in order
Only one ship per planet
Landing costs flight days
Players who land load indicated goods onto their ships

Abandoned Ship/Station

Only one player can use this opportunity
Leader decides first, then passes to next player if declined
Abandoned Ship: Give up crew for credits
Abandoned Station: Need minimum crew size to dock and collect goods
Both cost flight days

Enemies (Smugglers, Pirates, Slavers)

Attack players in order, starting with leader
Players compare cannon strength against enemy strength
If player wins: collect rewards (goods or credits) at cost of flight days
If player loses: suffer penalties (lose goods, crew, or get shot at)
If player ties: nothing happens, enemy moves to next player
Once an enemy is defeated, remaining players are safe

Open Space

Each player declares engine strength in order
Move forward that many empty spaces
Must have engine strength > 0 or give up

Meteor Swarm

Meteors come from indicated directions
Leader rolls dice to determine row/column for each meteor
Small meteors: Bounce off smooth sides, hit exposed connectors unless protected by shield (costs 1 battery)
Large meteors: Must be shot by a cannon in that column (from front) or in same/adjacent row/column (from sides/rear)
Destroyed components go to discard pile

Combat Zone

Penalties for weakest ship in different categories:

Fewest crew: Lose flight days
Weakest engines: Lose crew
Weakest cannons: Get shot at


Ties are broken by flight position (player farthest ahead suffers)

Special Events

Stardust: Lose 1 flight day per exposed connector
Epidemic: Remove 1 crew from every occupied cabin joined to another occupied cabin
Sabotage: Random component destroyed on ship with smallest crew

Ship Damage

Hit components are destroyed and placed in discard pile
If components are disconnected, they fall off and are also lost
If ship breaks into multiple pieces, choose one to pilot
Lost components cost 1 credit each at journey's end

Losing Flight Days

Move rocket back the indicated number of empty spaces
If multiple players lose flight days simultaneously, move in reverse order

Aliens

Require life support systems attached to cabins
Cannot be placed in starting cabin
Each ship can have at most 1 alien of each color
Take up space of 2 humans
Purple aliens: +2 to cannon strength (if cannon strength > 0)
Brown aliens: +2 to engine strength (if engine strength > 0)
If life support is destroyed, alien is returned to bank

Giving Up
Forced to Give Up:

Losing all humans
No engine strength in Open Space
Getting lapped by leader

Choose to Give Up:

Decision must be made before next adventure card is revealed
Sell goods at half price
Still pay penalty for lost components
No placement reward

Journey's End

Award credits for finish order
Award credits for fewest exposed connectors
Sell goods at indicated prices
Lose 1 credit per component lost along the way
Most credits wins, but any profit counts as a win

Additional Rules

Goods shortage: Players load in order, first come first served
If only one player remains, Combat Zone and Sabotage events are ignored
Rule violations discovered during flight: Remove components, pay 1 credit penalty
