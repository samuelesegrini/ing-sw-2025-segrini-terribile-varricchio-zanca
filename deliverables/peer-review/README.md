# Peer review documents

**Empty on purpose.** These two documents are reviews of *another team's* design, and that
team's material is not in this repository. Writing them from here would mean inventing
findings about work nobody has read, which is worse than handing in nothing — a review is
only worth anything if somebody actually did it.

`TEMPLATE.md` is the shape each one should take. Fill it in against the design you were
given and save it as `first-review.md` and `second-review.md`.

Requirement D5, `requirements.pdf` § 1.
